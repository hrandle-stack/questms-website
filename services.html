Services

What we do

Cost and contract services across the life of a scheme, for employers and for contractors. Each commission is led at director level.

A1

Development management

We work with developers from the first look at a site to the start on site. That covers the development appraisal itself, the cost and viability inputs to planning, and running the planning application with the design team and the planning consultant.

Once consent is in place we assemble the funding pack, deal with the lender and its monitoring surveyor, and put the design team appointments and the building contract in place so the scheme is ready to start.

Typical outputs

  • Site and development appraisals
  • Viability assessments and cost inputs
  • Planning application management
  • Design team appointments and management
  • Funding packs and lender liaison
  • Development agreements
A2

Cost planning and estimating

We price schemes from the first sketch. Early on that is an order of cost estimate on a single page, with the basis and the exclusions written underneath so nobody reads a number out of context.

As the design develops it becomes an elemental cost plan to NRM1, measured from the drawings, with a written cost plan report and benchmarking against published data and our own scheme records.

Typical outputs

  • Order of cost estimates
  • Elemental cost plans to NRM1
  • Cost plan reports
  • Benchmarking and cost models
  • Option appraisals and value engineering
A3

Procurement and tendering

We advise on the procurement route and the form of contract, then write the tender documents and the pricing schedules that go with them.

Returns are levelled line by line so the comparison is like for like, and the tender report ends with a recommendation the client can act on.

Typical outputs

  • Procurement strategy
  • Tender documents and pricing schedules
  • Contract sum analyses
  • Tender analysis and reports
  • Award recommendations
A4

Contracts and appointments

A large share of our work is the paperwork that holds a project together. We prepare building contracts on the JCT and NEC forms with schedules of amendments drafted to the project, and the documents that sit around them, working alongside the client's solicitors where a point needs legal advice.

For contractors we review the employer's proposed terms at tender and again before signing, and prepare the subcontracts that pass the right obligations down the supply chain. When a scheme changes hands part way through, we prepare the novations, order transfers and vesting arrangements that move the supply chain across.

Typical outputs

  • JCT Design and Build, Intermediate, Minor Works and Construction Management
  • NEC3 and NEC4 ECC
  • Schedules of amendments
  • Subcontracts, trade contracts and orders
  • Consultant appointments
  • Collateral warranties
  • Letters of intent
  • Deeds of novation and vesting agreements
A5

Employer's agent and contract administration

We act for the employer under JCT Design and Build, Intermediate and Minor Works contracts, and provide the commercial side of the construction manager's role under JCT Construction Management.

That means operating the contract as written: instructions, notices and certificates issued in the right form and at the right time, with the record kept behind each decision.

Typical outputs

  • Employer's agent
  • Contract administrator
  • Instructions and notices
  • Certificates and completion
  • The defects period
A6

Project management

We act as Project Manager under the NEC3 and NEC4 Engineering and Construction Contracts; our director holds ICE accreditation as an ECC Project Manager under both editions. On an NEC job that means running the contract's own procedures within its time limits: accepting the programme, managing early warnings and assessing compensation events as they arise.

On JCT schemes we provide client-side project management alongside the employer's agent or contract administrator role, coordinating the design team, the programme and the client's decisions so the contractor is never waiting on information.

Typical outputs

  • NEC ECC Project Manager
  • Programme review and acceptance
  • Early warnings and the risk register
  • Compensation event assessment
  • Client-side project management on JCT
A7

Post-contract commercial management

Monthly valuations, payment notices and pay less notices, issued inside the contractual timetable and presented net to net off the previous notice so they read across without argument.

Variations are priced and agreed as they happen, and the cost report carries a forecast final cost the client can rely on. By the time the scheme completes, most of the final account is already settled.

Typical outputs

  • Interim valuations
  • Payment and pay less notices
  • Change control
  • Cost reports and cost to complete
  • Final accounts
A8

Contractor services

We work as the commercial team for main contractors, on bids and on live jobs.

At tender that covers pricing, the Contractor's Proposals and the contract position. Once the job is on site it covers subcontract procurement and orders, subcontract valuations and notices, and the monthly contractor's report to the client.

Typical outputs

  • Tender pricing
  • Contractor's Proposals
  • Subcontract procurement and orders
  • Subcontract valuations and notices
  • Contractor's reports
A9

Distressed and stalled projects

When a contractor fails or a scheme stalls, the first job is to establish what has been built and paid for, and what it will cost to finish.

From there we take over the trade packages that can be kept, procure a completion contract for the rest, and report to the funder each month until the scheme is finished.

Typical outputs

  • Cost to complete
  • Funding drawdowns
  • Completion contract procurement
  • Novation and takeover of subcontracts
  • Funder and monitoring surveyor reporting
A10

Claims and disputes

We assess extension of time and loss and expense claims against the contract terms and the project records, and we prepare and answer final account claims.

Where a dispute goes to adjudication we provide the quantum work, review witness evidence on the commercial points and support the client's legal team.

Typical outputs

  • Extension of time assessment
  • Loss and expense
  • Final account disputes
  • Adjudication support
  • Quantum reports
A11

Reinstatement cost assessments

Rebuild valuations for buildings insurance, prepared by quantity surveyors who price construction every day. We work for freeholders, residents' management companies and managing agents.
