About Quest

Quest Management Services is a quantity surveying and commercial management consultancy in the West Midlands. We were set up in 2022 to give residential developers the cost and contract service of a large practice, with director-level attention on every commission.

Our work now covers contractors as well as developers, and runs from the first order of cost estimate through to final accounts and disputes. Most of it is in Birmingham and the West Midlands, with commissions in London, Nottingham, Coventry and Wiltshire. Commissions range from strip-out contracts of around £100,000 to residential schemes with a construction value of over £90m.

B

How we work

Four habits

We put it in writing

Every number has a basis and every decision has a record. When a scheme is questioned later, the file answers.

Notices go out on time

Payment and pay less notices are issued inside the contractual timetable, every month. Late paperwork is how most commercial arguments start.

Figures read across

Valuations run net to net off the last notice, and cost reports reconcile to what has actually been certified and paid.

We know both sides of the table

We act for employers and for contractors, so we know how the other party will read a document before it is sent.
