Reinstatement cost assessments

What would it cost to rebuild?

A reinstatement cost assessment gives your buildings insurance a declared value based on what it would cost to demolish, clear and rebuild the building today. Ours are prepared by quantity surveyors who price construction for a living.

B

Who it is for

Freeholders, residents' companies and managing agents

Most of our assessments are for apartment blocks and mixed-use buildings, instructed by managing agents on behalf of the freeholder or the residents' management company. We offer portfolio rates to agents with several buildings.

If the sum insured is too low, the insurer may apply average and pay a claim in proportion. If it is too high, you pay premium on cover you cannot use. We recommend a full assessment at least every three years, with index-linking in between.

What the assessment covers

  • Site inspection of the building, inside and out
  • Gross internal area measured from drawings and checked on site
  • Rebuild cost at current prices, using our construction cost records and BCIS data
  • Demolition, site clearance and temporary works
  • Professional and statutory fees
  • The rebuild period, for loss of rent or alternative accommodation cover
  • Historic fabric and conservation area allowances where they apply
  • High-rise building considerations under the Building Safety Act
  • Apportionment between residential and commercial parts
  • A written report stating the declared value
C

How it works

Three steps

  1. Instruction

    Send the address, any drawings or floor plans you hold, and the current sum insured. We confirm a fixed fee and a date.

  2. Inspection

    We visit the building, measure or check the areas and record the construction, finishes and services.

  3. Report

    You receive the report with the declared value, the basis of the assessment and the rebuild period, ready to pass to your broker.
