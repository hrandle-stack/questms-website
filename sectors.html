Sectors

Multi-unit residential

Most of our work is buildings with many homes or beds in them. Each type has its own cost drivers and its own way of going wrong, and we know them from the inside. We also work across education, hotels, offices, leisure and self storage.

S1

Clusters, studios, conversions to PBSA

Student accommodation

Cost per bed is the figure everyone quotes, but the mix of cluster flats and studios, the amenity offer and the furniture package move it further than the frame does.

The programme is set by the academic year. A missed September opening can cost a full year of rent, so we look at phased handover and the cost of acceleration early, while there is still time to buy it.

Our schemes

  • 12 Calthorpe Road262 beds, office to residential conversion
S2

Investor-held apartments, operational blocks

Build to rent and PRS

An investor holding a building for twenty years cares about running cost and lifecycle as much as the capital cost. We price the operator's specification, the amenity space and the plant strategy with that hold period in mind.

We also work on let buildings once they are operating, where replacing plant or carrying out remedial work has to be done around residents.

Our schemes

  • Cliveland House52-apartment private rented block
S3

City centre apartments, townhouse schemes

Apartments and townhouses for sale

Build to sell runs to the sales programme and the funding facility. We value monthly against the facility, prepare the drawdowns and keep the cost to complete current, so the developer always knows the headroom.

Phased handover, warranty inspections and the defects period are planned into the contract from the start, because that is where the final account is won or lost.

  • Gunsmith House85 apartments, including restoration of Gunsmith House
  • Heaton House14 townhouses and 42 apartments
  • Imperial House60 apartments, high-rise building
  • Sapcote Yard13 townhouses on a former builder's yard
  • Tenby Street39 new-build homes
  • Trent Bridge Quays Phase IIRiverside townhouses and apartments
S4

Extra care, care homes, later living

Retirement villages and care

Later living carries far more communal and care space per unit than general needs housing, along with specialist services such as nurse call, assisted bathing and commercial kitchens. We benchmark per bed and per m² GIA, against external and internal data.

Our experience includes several extra care retirement villages, from cost planning through tender.

Our schemes

  • Tower Mount80-bed care home, 4,095 m² GIA
S5

Offices, laboratories, listed and conservation buildings

Conversions and heritage

Much of Birmingham's new housing is going into old buildings. Conversions carry their risk in the existing structure, so we price surveys and opening-up works early and hold a realistic contingency until the building has been stripped back.

Historic fabric and conservation area consents bring their own cost and programme, and we allow for both.

Our schemes

  • 12 Calthorpe Road262 beds, office to residential conversion
  • Gunsmith House85 apartments, including restoration of Gunsmith House
  • No. 30 St Paul's7,177 m² GIA, apartments, commercial units and basement car park
  • 32 Ravenscourt GardensVictorian terrace to three dwellings with a new basement
S6

Insolvency, termination, completion contracts

Stalled and distressed schemes

When the builder has gone, the work is forensic first: establishing what has been built and paid for, and which subcontracts are worth keeping. Then a completion contract, priced and let on terms the funder will accept.

This work is confidential by nature, so we do not name the schemes. We are happy to talk through relevant experience in confidence.

Our schemes

  • Available in confidence
S7

Schools, academies, colleges, publicly funded programmes

Education and public sector

Education has been a large part of our team's work: new schools, extensions and refurbishments for the Education Funding Agency, among them schemes that had to be recovered and brought back on programme.

School programmes are fixed by the academic year and the funder's approvals. Work in occupied schools is phased around term dates and safeguarding, and the cost plan carries that from the first estimate.

Today we also act for contractors on education work, from the Contractor's Proposals through to the building contract.

Experience

  • Schools for the Education Funding Agency
  • New build schools, extensions and refurbishments
  • Recovery of schemes that had fallen behind
  • Sixth form college extension and refurbishment, Worcestershire

Beyond residential and education

Other sectors

There is very little in building that we have not worked on. These are the other sectors our team works in.

S8

Full service and select service hotels

Hotels

Hotel cost plans turn on the key count, the brand standard and the operator's technical requirements, with the FF&E and OS&E budgets carried as separate lines the operator will hold us to.

Our team has run the hotels department of a national cost consultancy, so we know how brand standards and operator sign-offs drive cost.

Experience

  • Hotels department, national cost consultancy
  • Full service and select service hotels
S9

New build offices, Cat A and Cat B fit-out, strip-out

Offices and commercial

From city centre office towers to a single floor fit-out, the cost sits in the frame and facade on new build and in the services on fit-out. We cost plan, procure and administer both.

Strip-out and soft strip contracts carry their own risks, from asbestos to isolations, and we write those into the contract before work starts.

Experience

  • Three Snow Hill, Birmingham
  • Cat B office fit-out, Birmingham
  • Westwood Park soft strip, Coventry
S10

Arenas, leisure and mixed-use

Leisure and arenas

Large leisure buildings bring long-span structures, complex services and event-led programmes. Our experience includes leading the commercial team on arena, leisure and mixed-use residential schemes.

Experience

  • Arena and leisure schemes
  • Mixed-use leisure and residential

Self storage, warehouses, trade units

Self storage and industrial

Self storage is a fast build with a lot of repetition and a heavy dependence on the mezzanine and the cladding packages. We act for contractors on building contracts, appointments and steelwork procurement.
