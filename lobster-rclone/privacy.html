Lobster rclone

Privacy policy

Lobster rclone is a private application used only by its owner, Harry Randle of Quest Management Services Ltd. This policy explains how it handles Google user data.

Whose data

The app is used only with the owner's own Google account. It is not available to other users and it does not access, collect or store information about anyone other than the owner.

What the app accesses

With the owner's permission, the app accesses the owner's Google Drive through the Google Drive API. It uses that access only to copy the owner's own files from the owner's Dropbox account into the owner's Google Drive, and to check that the copies are complete.

Storage and sharing

The sign-in token issued by Google is stored only on the owner's own computer. No Google user data is sold, shared, transferred to third parties, or used for advertising, analytics or any purpose other than the backup described above.

The use and transfer of information received from Google APIs by this app adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can withdraw the app's access at any time from the Google Account permissions page at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and can delete the stored token from the computer.
