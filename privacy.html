Privacy notice

This notice explains how Quest Management Services Ltd (company number 14389700, 12 Payton Street, Stratford-upon-Avon CV37 6UA) handles personal information. We are the controller for the information described here.

This website

This website does not use cookies, analytics or tracking, and it has no forms. Our hosting provider keeps standard server logs for security, which may include your IP address. We do not use them to identify visitors.

When you contact us

If you email or phone us we keep your name, contact details and what you tell us, so that we can reply and, if you instruct us, carry out the work. Our lawful basis is our legitimate interest in responding to enquiries and, where you instruct us, the performance of a contract. Project records are kept for as long as our professional and contractual obligations require, which for construction work is normally up to twelve years after completion.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it. Write to harry@questms.co.uk. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
